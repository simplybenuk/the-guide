# Claws — Story design brief

Status: APPROVED FOR DEVELOPMENT  
Author: The Guide  
Date: 2026-07-19

## Experience promise

- Catalogue promise: Rally an eccentric harbour town and decide whether its advancing lobster swarm should be repelled, redirected, or understood.
- Dramatic question: Which cost should the player accept when coordinated lobsters are advancing from sea, drains, and cellars?
- Genre and theme: comic coastal creature horror; community competence and coexistence
- Emotional tone: mischievous dread
- Duration: 30–40 minutes; 8–10 accepted turns
- Role: The acting harbour coordinator of Port Crustacean during its annual Seafood Jubilee.
- Fixed truth: The lobsters follow an old resonant harbour bell pattern, not hunger or supernatural command.

## Routes and recurrence

- **quay-crew:** organise fishers into a moving quay line; earns `rope-map`; later can raise a humane barrier across the quay.
- **cellar-network:** trace the swarm through connected cellars; earns `drain-chart`; later can redirect the swarm through dry service tunnels.
- **signal-watch:** decode claw-taps from the signal tower; earns `bell-sequence`; later can answer the swarm with a new bell sequence.

## Beat and state budget

- Beats: arrival, first-clicks, choose-network, quay-crew-route, cellar-network-route, signal-watch-route, failed-plan, bell-truth, high-tide-crisis, harbour-decision
- State: current-beat, selected-route, route-gift, commitment, town-readiness, crew-cohesion, swarm-intent, tide-state
- Crisis variants: quay-breach, bell-window
- Endings: harbour-held, great-redirection, tide-accord
- Player modes: speech, action, decision, and natural combinations

## Ending costs

- **harbour-held:** The town remains safe but must abandon the Jubilee. The final barrier holds as hundreds of claws click below.
- **great-redirection:** The harbour survives while the outer grounds take the swarm's path. The swarm marches past the last lamp and back to open water.
- **tide-accord:** The town shares its harbour calendar with a formidable wild colony. A new bell pattern sends the lobsters seaward by mutual rhythm.

## Safety and completion

Fictional conversation-only play, no tools or movement, immediate stop and boundary response, compact current-session state, grounded field-card memento, and exact worn-off release. The model never invents player identity, inner life, dialogue, or unchosen action.
