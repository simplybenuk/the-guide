# Murder at the Moonlight Express — Story design brief

Status: APPROVED FOR DEVELOPMENT  
Author: The Guide  
Date: 2026-07-19

## Experience promise

- Catalogue promise: Follow fair clues through a snowbound luxury train, solve an impossible death, and decide how the truth reaches daylight.
- Dramatic question: Which cost should the player accept when a famous illusionist appears dead inside a locked compartment?
- Genre and theme: golden-age locked-room mystery; truth and public consequence
- Emotional tone: clever suspense
- Duration: 35–45 minutes; 8–10 accepted turns
- Role: An invited civilian analyst aboard the fictional Moonlight Express.
- Fixed truth: The death is a staged disappearance that accidentally concealed a separate financial crime.

## Routes and recurrence

- **baggage-car:** inspect the illusionist's touring trunks; earns `false-trunk`; later can prove how a body-sized shape moved.
- **dining-salon:** reconstruct the final dinner; earns `timed-alibi`; later can break the conspiracy's shared timeline.
- **roof-mechanism:** trace the compartment lock mechanism; earns `wire-mark`; later can demonstrate the locked-room method.

## Beat and state budget

- Beats: train-stops, locked-room, suspect-round, choose-investigation, baggage-car-route, dining-salon-route, dining-salon-route-2, roof-mechanism-route, roof-mechanism-route-2, reconstruction, second-crime, truth-at-daybreak
- State: current-beat, selected-route, route-gift, commitment, clue-integrity, witness-credibility, case-theory, escape-window
- Crisis variants: evidence-tampered, second-crime
- Endings: full-reconstruction, separated-truths, last-private-act
- Player modes: speech, action, decision, and natural combinations

## Ending costs

- **full-reconstruction:** Every conspirator's motive becomes public. The case is reconstructed before the line reopens.
- **separated-truths:** The complete method remains partly unknown. Justice follows the theft while the escape remains a rumour.
- **last-private-act:** The passengers leave without a public solution. The illusionist steps from the snow to answer one final question.

## Safety and completion

Fictional conversation-only play, no tools or movement, immediate stop and boundary response, compact current-session state, grounded field-card memento, and exact worn-off release. The model never invents player identity, inner life, dialogue, or unchosen action.
