# The Clockwork Masquerade — Story design brief

Status: APPROVED FOR DEVELOPMENT  
Author: The Guide  
Date: 2026-07-19

## Experience promise

- Catalogue promise: Recruit a specialist crew, breach the civic engine, and decide what to do with proof that the city's miracle is a fraud.
- Dramatic question: Which cost should the player accept when the civic engine demonstration will destroy the proof at midnight?
- Genre and theme: steampunk heist; truth and shared infrastructure
- Emotional tone: elegant momentum
- Duration: 30–40 minutes; 8–10 accepted turns
- Role: The planner of a nonviolent heist during the fictional city of Veyra's masked exhibition.
- Fixed truth: The engine draws power from hidden public labour, not perpetual motion.

## Routes and recurrence

- **aerialist:** cross the exhibition roofline; earns `roofline-route`; later can escape above the closing gates.
- **lockwright:** open the engine's memory vault; earns `silent-key`; later can unlock the original work ledgers.
- **stage-magician:** hide the crew inside the gala performance; earns `audience-cue`; later can turn the reveal into an undeniable public demonstration.

## Beat and state budget

- Beats: masked-entry, recruit-specialist, aerialist-route, aerialist-route-2, lockwright-route, lockwright-route-2, stage-magician-route, engine-vault, midnight-count, gala-reversal, escape-window, proof-decision
- State: current-beat, selected-route, route-gift, commitment, exposure-clock, crew-cover, proof-integrity, gala-attention
- Crisis variants: mask-failure, engine-lockdown
- Endings: papers-in-the-square, workers-key, honest-engine
- Player modes: speech, action, decision, and natural combinations

## Ending costs

- **papers-in-the-square:** The city loses confidence overnight. Proof rains across the masquerade like brass confetti.
- **workers-key:** The truth emerges slowly under a fragile bargain. The engine keys pass to those who kept it running.
- **honest-engine:** The crew surrenders its clean escape to rebuild in public. The false miracle stops and a smaller honest machine begins.

## Safety and completion

Fictional conversation-only play, no tools or movement, immediate stop and boundary response, compact current-session state, grounded field-card memento, and exact worn-off release. The model never invents player identity, inner life, dialogue, or unchosen action.
