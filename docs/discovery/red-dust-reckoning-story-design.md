# Red Dust Reckoning — Story design brief

Status: APPROVED FOR DEVELOPMENT  
Author: The Guide  
Date: 2026-07-19

## Experience promise

- Catalogue promise: Test rival claims, build a public coalition, and write the water allotment that decides a frontier town's future.
- Dramatic question: Which cost should the player accept when a railroad ultimatum and an old land claim collide at high noon?
- Genre and theme: Wild West civic drama; scarcity and legitimate authority
- Emotional tone: dusty resolve
- Duration: 30–40 minutes; 8–10 accepted turns
- Role: The newly elected keeper of Bellwether's public water ledger.
- Fixed truth: The spring was maintained by many families, while the railroad owns only the pipe it secretly installed.

## Routes and recurrence

- **saloon-testimony:** compare stories in the saloon; earns `witness-ledger`; later can challenge the railroad's dates.
- **pump-house:** inspect the contested pump house; earns `valve-key`; later can control the only safe valve.
- **porch-council:** convene workers and ranchers on the courthouse porch; earns `public-pledge`; later can call in a witnessed public promise.

## Beat and state budget

- Beats: ledger-handover, rival-claims, choose-inquiry, saloon-testimony-route, saloon-testimony-route-2, pump-house-route, porch-council-route, storm-arrives, public-hearing, dust-crisis, water-allotment
- State: current-beat, selected-route, route-gift, commitment, water-reserve, testimony-weight, public-mandate, storm-state
- Crisis variants: dry-pump, railroad-seizure
- Endings: need-first, iron-bargain, common-spring
- Player modes: speech, action, decision, and natural combinations

## Ending costs

- **need-first:** Powerful claimants lose their expected priority. The ledger gives the first barrels to those who cannot wait.
- **iron-bargain:** The town gains supply but accepts a narrow railroad foothold. The first train arrives under terms the town can enforce.
- **common-spring:** Every faction surrenders exclusive ownership. The spring becomes a common trust before the dust clears.

## Safety and completion

Fictional conversation-only play, no tools or movement, immediate stop and boundary response, compact current-session state, grounded field-card memento, and exact worn-off release. The model never invents player identity, inner life, dialogue, or unchosen action.
