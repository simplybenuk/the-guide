# Neon Saints — Story design brief

Status: APPROVED FOR DEVELOPMENT  
Author: The Guide  
Date: 2026-07-19

## Experience promise

- Catalogue promise: Cross a neon city with an encrypted recollection and decide who, if anyone, can own another person's past.
- Dramatic question: Which cost should the player accept when three factions claim the sealed memory and the courier licence is expiring?
- Genre and theme: cyberpunk noir; identity and ownership
- Emotional tone: rainy defiance
- Duration: 30–40 minutes; 8–10 accepted turns
- Role: A licensed memory courier carrying one sealed fictional recollection.
- Fixed truth: The memory was voluntarily copied but its future ownership was never granted.

## Routes and recurrence

- **street-clinic:** verify the memory's consent signature; earns `consent-key`; later can let the original owner revoke access.
- **corporate-arcade:** follow its ownership audit; earns `audit-trace`; later can prove the buyer changed the contract.
- **pirate-radio:** speak with an underground archivist; earns `broadcast-window`; later can release only a chosen fragment publicly.

## Beat and state budget

- Beats: courier-brief, three-claims, choose-faction, street-clinic-route, street-clinic-route-2, street-clinic-route-3, corporate-arcade-route, pirate-radio-route, consent-record, licence-expires, dawn-crisis, memory-delivery
- State: current-beat, selected-route, route-gift, commitment, licence-clock, faction-trust, consent-status, copy-spread
- Crisis variants: licence-expiry, memory-leak
- Endings: memory-home, consented-delivery, no-one-owns-dawn
- Player modes: speech, action, decision, and natural combinations

## Ending costs

- **memory-home:** The courier loses the promised fee. The recollection returns to the person who made it.
- **consented-delivery:** A valuable memory enters use under strict limits. The sealed memory opens under terms its owner can revoke.
- **no-one-owns-dawn:** The city loses evidence contained only in the copies. Every market copy goes dark as morning reaches the street.

## Safety and completion

Fictional conversation-only play, no tools or movement, immediate stop and boundary response, compact current-session state, grounded field-card memento, and exact worn-off release. The model never invents player identity, inner life, dialogue, or unchosen action.
