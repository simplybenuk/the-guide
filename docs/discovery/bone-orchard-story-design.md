# The Bone Orchard — Story design brief

Status: APPROVED FOR DEVELOPMENT  
Author: The Guide  
Date: 2026-07-19

## Experience promise

- Catalogue promise: Keep an ancient village boundary, uncover why a living name appeared, and decide whether its harvest bargain should survive.
- Dramatic question: Which cost should the player accept when one white-barked tree bears a living villager's name?
- Genre and theme: folk horror; tradition and chosen remembrance
- Emotional tone: autumnal unease
- Duration: 35–45 minutes; 8–10 accepted turns
- Role: The newly appointed boundary-keeper of the fictional village of Rill.
- Fixed truth: The orchard records people the village has socially erased, not people destined to die.

## Routes and recurrence

- **root-cellar:** read names beneath the oldest roots; earns `first-carving`; later can identify the first deliberate erasure.
- **family-table:** hear three families dispute the living name; earns `missing-place`; later can restore a place at the harvest table.
- **boundary-stones:** walk the orchard's marked boundary; earns `older-bargain`; later can invoke the bargain that predates the current ritual.

## Beat and state budget

- Beats: orchard-gate, living-name, choose-witness, root-cellar-route, family-table-route, family-table-route-2, family-table-route-3, boundary-stones-route, harvest-table, older-bargain, harvest-crisis, name-the-grove
- State: current-beat, selected-route, route-gift, commitment, village-memory, family-standing, bargain-form, harvest-pressure
- Crisis variants: name-spreads, harvest-begins
- Endings: truthful-roots, felled-bargain, chosen-grove
- Player modes: speech, action, decision, and natural combinations

## Ending costs

- **truthful-roots:** The village must name harms it preferred to forget. The living name fades only after its place is restored.
- **felled-bargain:** The dead lose a familiar communal ritual. The white tree falls and no new name appears.
- **chosen-grove:** Some families refuse the new voluntary remembrance. The grove remains, but no name is carved without consent.

## Safety and completion

Fictional conversation-only play, no tools or movement, immediate stop and boundary response, compact current-session state, grounded field-card memento, and exact worn-off release. The model never invents player identity, inner life, dialogue, or unchosen action.
