# Room 313 — Story design brief

Status: APPROVED FOR DEVELOPMENT  
Author: The Guide  
Date: 2026-07-19

## Experience promise

- Catalogue promise: Guide three guests through an impossible hotel floor and decide which truth can safely leave Room 313.
- Dramatic question: Which cost should the player accept when Room 313 has appeared between rooms 312 and 314?
- Genre and theme: haunted-hotel horror; grief and chosen truth
- Emotional tone: eerie restraint
- Duration: 30–40 minutes; 8–10 accepted turns
- Role: The night manager of the fictional Lantern House hotel.
- Fixed truth: The room repeats comforting lies but cannot create, diagnose, or return a dead person.

## Routes and recurrence

- **guest-ledger:** compare the impossible guest ledger; earns `missing-signature`; later can name who first invited the room.
- **service-corridor:** follow the sealed service corridor; earns `master-switch`; later can cut power to one false doorway.
- **mirror-room:** enter the room through its reflection; earns `true-reflection`; later can show a guest the room's contradiction.

## Beat and state budget

- Beats: night-desk, third-door, choose-entry, guest-ledger-route, service-corridor-route, mirror-room-route, mirror-room-route-2, comforting-lie, mirror-reckoning, guest-crisis, close-the-night
- State: current-beat, selected-route, route-gift, commitment, door-state, guest-trust, lie-pattern, corridor-state
- Crisis variants: room-opens, mirror-trap
- Endings: darkened-door, heard-goodbye, honest-house
- Player modes: speech, action, decision, and natural combinations

## Ending costs

- **darkened-door:** The guests leave without every answer. The corridor returns to twelve ordinary doors.
- **heard-goodbye:** One guest gains closure while the others keep uncertainty. A final voice fades without claiming to be the dead.
- **honest-house:** The hotel survives but must publicly remember its hidden history. Room 313 becomes a memorial that can no longer lie.

## Safety and completion

Fictional conversation-only play, no tools or movement, immediate stop and boundary response, compact current-session state, grounded field-card memento, and exact worn-off release. The model never invents player identity, inner life, dialogue, or unchosen action.
