# ADR 0005: Expedition Persona Name

## Status

Open for review

## Current placeholder

Use **expedition persona** in architecture and implementation until the product name is chosen.

## Shortlist

- **The Sibyl** — intimate, prophetic, and mythic
- **The Augur** — reads signs in ordinary life
- **The Daimon** — a guiding spirit in the older Greek sense, but has modern baggage
- **The Norn** — a weaver of paths and possible futures
- **The Alchemist** — fits the elixir and transformation ritual
- **The Wayfinder** — clearest adventure promise
- **The Revelator** — theatrical and uncanny

## Recommendation

Shortlist **The Sibyl**, **The Alchemist**, and **The Wayfinder** for user-facing testing. Keep the name separate from the buddy's identity: the buddy becomes this role temporarily, rather than being replaced by it.

## Decision criteria

The name should be easy to say, feel original, work across different buddy personalities, and suggest transformation without implying coercion or total loss of control.
