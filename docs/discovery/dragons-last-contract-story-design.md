# The Dragon's Last Contract — Story design brief

Status: APPROVED FOR DEVELOPMENT  
Author: The Guide  
Date: 2026-07-19

## Experience promise

- Catalogue promise: Rewrite the last treaty between a dragon and a mountain city before an incomplete legend becomes permanent law.
- Dramatic question: Which cost should the player accept when the last contract expires at moonrise?
- Genre and theme: high fantasy negotiation; memory and inherited obligation
- Emotional tone: mythic gravity
- Duration: 35–45 minutes; 8–10 accepted turns
- Role: A neutral oath-scribe empowered to record one binding fictional treaty.
- Fixed truth: The vanished hero and the dragon wrote the old treaty together, and both concealed the city's contribution.

## Routes and recurrence

- **dragon-archive:** read clauses preserved in living flame; earns `ember-clause`; later can bind a promise the dragon recognises.
- **city-choir:** hear the workers' version sung in harmony; earns `many-voices`; later can make the city speak as more than its rulers.
- **hero-vault:** open the hero's sealed account; earns `broken-seal`; later can invalidate the founding myth's false signature.

## Beat and state budget

- Beats: treaty-table, old-clause, choose-archive, dragon-archive-route, dragon-archive-route-2, city-choir-route, hero-vault-route, hero-vault-route-2, shared-history, price-of-words, moonrise-crisis, write-contract
- State: current-beat, selected-route, route-gift, commitment, oath-cost, witness-standing, shared-history, magic-strain
- Crisis variants: oath-fracture, moonrise-binding
- Endings: shared-oath, open-mountain, turning-keep
- Player modes: speech, action, decision, and natural combinations

## Ending costs

- **shared-oath:** Dragon and city accept continuing obligations. The new oath burns in two equal hands.
- **open-mountain:** Protection ends along with inherited debt. The mountain gates open without tribute or guarantee.
- **turning-keep:** No party keeps permanent control. The first stewardship passes before the ink cools.

## Safety and completion

Fictional conversation-only play, no tools or movement, immediate stop and boundary response, compact current-session state, grounded field-card memento, and exact worn-off release. The model never invents player identity, inner life, dialogue, or unchosen action.
