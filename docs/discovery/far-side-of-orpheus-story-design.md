# The Far Side of Orpheus — Story design brief

Status: APPROVED FOR DEVELOPMENT  
Author: The Guide  
Date: 2026-07-19

## Experience promise

- Catalogue promise: Hold a divided crew together and decide whether to close, preserve, or exploit a message from the ship's near future.
- Dramatic question: Which cost should the player accept when a distress call arrives in the crew's voices from twelve minutes ahead?
- Genre and theme: hard science fiction time-loop drama; trust and responsibility
- Emotional tone: austere wonder
- Duration: 35–45 minutes; 8–10 accepted turns
- Role: The commander of the fictional research vessel Orpheus behind a silent moon.
- Fixed truth: The signal is a causal echo produced by the experimental drive and can be changed only once.

## Routes and recurrence

- **engine-room:** enter the unstable drive chamber; earns `phase-window`; later can close the loop at one precise phase.
- **listening-array:** isolate voices inside the echo; earns `echo-map`; later can preserve one clean scientific record.
- **crew-council:** let the divided crew set red lines; earns `consent-protocol`; later can require consent before any risky transmission.

## Beat and state budget

- Beats: damage-wake, future-echo, crew-split, choose-specialist, engine-room-route, listening-array-route, listening-array-route-2, crew-council-route, twelve-minute-mark, paradox-crisis, final-transmission
- State: current-beat, selected-route, route-gift, commitment, power-reserve, specialist-confidence, loop-phase, crew-consent
- Crisis variants: echo-collapse, causal-lock
- Endings: closed-orbit, witness-signal, chosen-paradox
- Player modes: speech, action, decision, and natural combinations

## Ending costs

- **closed-orbit:** The unique phenomenon is lost. Orpheus returns to ordinary time with empty instruments.
- **witness-signal:** The crew keeps evidence but accepts an uncertain escape. The echo becomes a truthful record rather than a command.
- **chosen-paradox:** The ship survives by creating the very warning it feared. The final message leaves exactly twelve minutes before it arrives.

## Safety and completion

Fictional conversation-only play, no tools or movement, immediate stop and boundary response, compact current-session state, grounded field-card memento, and exact worn-off release. The model never invents player identity, inner life, dialogue, or unchosen action.
